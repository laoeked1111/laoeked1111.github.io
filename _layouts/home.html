02 — About

About me

I'm studying EECS and physics as an undergraduate in MIT's class of 2028. Currently, I am an EE intern at Ouster working across the discipline by designing wireless power systems, laying out PCBs in Altium, automating hardware tests, simulating circuits, and soldering an excessive amount of tiny components.

At MIT, I am an undergraduate researcher at the Wellman Center for Photomedicine, where I am facilitating the development of optical coherence elastography for medical imaging.

This site is a portfolio of the things I've built -- feel free to get in touch.
