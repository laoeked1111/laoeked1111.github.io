I designed a PCB business card in KiCad. 
The front features a QR code to my LinkedIn, imperial and metric rulers, and an NFC IC connected to a planar coil that can be programmed (e.g. to link to my portfolio).
The back features various commonly used SMD footprints.
The design files are in [this Github repository](https://github.com/laoeked1111/pcb-business-card).
